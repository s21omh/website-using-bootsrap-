Integrated Pest Management

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) combines different approaches to manage agricultural pests.

  1. Monitor crops regularly.
  2. Detect signs of pest activity.
  3. Identify the pest problem.
  4. Consider suitable control methods.
  5. Monitor the results.

Treatment Strategies

Effective treatment begins with monitoring and early detection of pest infestations.

Regularly inspect crops and observe changes that may indicate pest activity.

Consider biological control methods as part of an integrated pest management approach.

Organic remedies can be considered as one approach for managing pest infestations.

Pest Management Tips

🔎 Inspect Regularly

Regular crop inspection helps identify possible pest problems at an early stage.

🌱 Monitor Crop Health

Observe crop health and look for signs that may indicate pest activity.

📋 Choose Carefully

Select suitable pest-management strategies based on the identified problem.
