Step-by-Step Farming Guide

Follow the basic stages of crop production.

Prepare the land properly before planting. Make the soil suitable for the selected crop and remove unwanted materials.

Select suitable seeds according to the crop type, season, farming method, and local growing conditions.

Plant the selected crop using an appropriate planting method and maintain suitable growing conditions.

Regularly monitor the crop and manage water, soil, weeds, pests, and other crop-care requirements.

Monitor crop maturity and use an appropriate harvesting method to collect the crop effectively.

Farming Tips & Best Practices

🌱 Choose Suitable Crops

💧 Monitor Water

Pay attention to crop water requirements and maintain suitable growing conditions.

🔍 Inspect Crops

Regularly observe crops to identify possible problems at an early stage.
